Culture

International Night

November 10th, 2026 | 5:30 PM | Main Hub Ballroom

Experience performances from various cultures and countries, featuring traditional music, dance, and more!

Event Description

The most important event of the year for celebrating diversity and cultural exchange on campus! Experience tranditional music, dance, and food from around the world along with cultural performances and activities! Maybe you'll even make some new connections too!

What to Expect

This is an indoor, seated event. Wear comfortable clothing and be prepared for a night of cultural celebration. Seating is available in the Main Hub Ballroom, and there will be plenty of space to move around and enjoy the performances.

Schedule

  • 5:30 PM — Doors open and welcome reception
  • 6:00 PM — Cultural performances and food tasting
  • 7:00 PM — Break
  • 7:15 PM — More cultural performances
  • 8:15 PM — Closing remarks and thank yous
  • 8:30 PM — Event concludes

Accessibility Participation

This event is fully accessible to all attendees. A student can also bring a guest. There are ways to access the event without going up or down stairs. There is also officers willing to help if asked. If you have any specific accessibility needs, please contact the Office of Student Engagement at least 72 hours in advance.
