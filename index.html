About University of Tú events

University of Tú Events is maintained by the Office of Student Engagement to help students find out what's happening across campus in one place, instead of digging flyers that don't stay on the walls for long, or searching through multiple social media platforms. The events that gain the most traction will be one that shines the most!

Whether you're looking to build your resume, network, stay curious and learn about various subjects, there is a event for you to stop buy! Don't use all your tuition money on just classes alone! Use it to form your community!
